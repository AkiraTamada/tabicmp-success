How it works

3 steps to find
the cheapest fare

No complex setup. Just install and start saving prices immediately.

1
Install the extension
Add TabiCmp from the Chrome Web Store in under a minute — free. Sign in with Google to sync your saved prices.
2
Bookmark with ⭐
A ⭐ button appears next to each fare on supported sites. Tap it to save any price you want to compare.
3
Compare in the side panel
All your saved fares appear in the side panel. The cheapest is highlighted automatically — across every site.
